How to read this

Each review's weighted score is modelled as score = overall mean + judge leniency + judge scale × project quality + noise. Leniency and scale are estimated jointly with project quality from every overlapping review, and shrunk toward "average judge" in proportion to how little evidence there is, so a judge with one review is barely adjusted and a judge with ten is adjusted as far as their data supports. The adjusted score is what an average judge would be expected to give. The 90% rank range and P(top {{$r.TopK}}) come from {{$r.Bootstrap}} bootstrap resamples of each project's reviews: if two projects' ranges overlap, the data does not separate them. Full derivation in JUDGING.md.
